## 🛠️ Built With
- **HTML5 & CSS3**
- **JavaScript (ES6+)**
- **Bootstrap 5**
- FontAwesome Icons & Google Fonts

## 📱 Sections
- **Hero & About Me:** Professional background and engineering goals.
- **Projects Showcase:** Interactive cards linking to active codebases.
- **Technical Skills:** Frontend, Backend, and Tooling proficiencies.
- **Certificates & Achievements:** Badges and verified credentials.
- **Contact:** Quick social and email contact links.
